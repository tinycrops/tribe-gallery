Predicted fMRI cortical activation for the first 15 seconds of each video, using Meta's TRIBE v2 multimodal brain encoding model.

What am I looking at? TRIBE v2 predicts how a brain would respond (fMRI BOLD signal) while watching each video. It combines three feature extractors — V-JEPA2 (video), Wav2Vec-BERT (audio), LLaMA 3.2 (transcribed speech) — then maps those representations onto 20,484 vertices of the cortical surface (fsaverage5). The line charts below show mean predicted activation across the Destrieux cortical atlas (76 regions per hemisphere) over 15 seconds. Chips show the top-5 most activated regions. Positive values = activation above resting baseline. For guitar content: expect auditory cortex (STG) + visual cortex (early visual areas) to dominate.
